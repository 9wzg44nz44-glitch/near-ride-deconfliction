Scenario: riders and departure times

Set riders per route (0 = not riding) and departure times in local Eastern clock time, 7:00 AM to 3:00 PM. Defaults: SBH Monadnock 18 riders at 9:00 AM, MBH Monadnock 7 riders at 9:30 AM. Any change recalculates the simulation and returns the clock to the start; then press Run above.

Click a row to jump the map and clock to that moment. An encounter means a rider of one group is within the threshold distance (default 30 m) of a rider of another group, measured on the map, so shared roads and crossings both count.

Start-time optimizer

Searches departure times between 7:00 AM and 3:00 PM in 5 minute steps for the groups above. Locked groups keep their departure time. Severity weights Class VI overtakes and head-on meetings most heavily and paved encounters least. Schedules must also stay clear when either group is up to 15 minutes early or late.

Leader briefing and encounter forecast

Runs the scenario many times with random speed variation (about 6% per group plus local variation), stop-time variation and a start jitter of about 2 minutes, then reports how often each encounter happens and when.

Reality check / calibrate

      
Enter something you actually saw: which group, at which mile marker of which route, and about when. The tool shows where and when the model has that group, the offset or pace the model would need to match your sighting, and which assumptions disagree. Sightings appear on the map as cyan markers. Hover a route on the map to read its mile marker; with "Click map to fill" on, a click fills the route and mile.

      

        
        
        
        
        
        
      

      

        
        
        
        
      

      

    


    

      
Terrain editor

      
OpenStreetMap tags are not perfect. Pick a route, then click two points on that route on the map (or type mile values), choose the terrain and apply. Edits are saved in this browser and can be exported or imported as JSON.

Stops & waypoints

      
Regroup stops come from the gas and food waypoints in the GPX files. Default rule: the first stop with food is the lunch stop (45 min), every other gas or food stop is a short stop (15 min). Change any value (0 = skip). Stop signs, signals and other controls come from OpenStreetMap; some are inferred (see About).

Add your own GPX route

      
The file is read in your browser only (nothing is uploaded). Without OpenStreetMap data, the whole route gets one default terrain which you then refine in the Terrain editor. Added routes live in this browser session (and are kept in local storage if small enough). To add routes permanently, run the build scripts in the repository.
